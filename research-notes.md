# Research Notes: Engagement + Learning Science

## TikTok/Reels Engagement Mechanics
1. **Variable Ratio Reinforcement** — Algorithm intentionally mixes high/medium/low quality content to create unpredictable reward patterns (slot machine effect)
2. **Infinite Vertical Scroll** — Full-screen immersive cards, swipe-to-next removes friction
3. **Micro-content (15-60s)** — Low commitment threshold, high completion rates
4. **Dopamine Loop** — Anticipation → Reward → Repeat cycle; each swipe is a new "pull"
5. **Personalization Signals** — Watch time, replays, shares, follows; real-time adaptation
6. **Flow State Design** — Minimal UI chrome, full-screen content, no visible end point
7. **Social Proof** — View counts, likes, comments create FOMO and validation
8. **Sound-On Default** — Audio engagement increases retention

## Learning Science Principles
1. **Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve** — 50% forgotten in 1 hour, 70% in 24 hours without review
2. **Spaced Repetition (SM-2/Leitner)** — Review at increasing intervals: 1 day → 3 days → 7 days → 14 days → 30 days
3. **Active Recall** — Testing > re-reading; retrieval strengthens memory traces
4. **Interleaving** — Mixing topics > blocking; forces discrimination and deeper processing
5. **Desirable Difficulties (Bjork)** — Spacing, interleaving, retrieval practice, generation effect
6. **Zone of Proximal Development (Vygotsky)** — Content just beyond current knowledge
7. **Flow State (Csikszentmihalyi)** — Balance challenge and skill; clear goals, immediate feedback
8. **Microlearning** — 3-7 minute chunks; reduces cognitive load, improves completion
9. **Elaborative Interrogation** — "Why does this work?" prompts deeper encoding
10. **Generation Effect** — Self-generated answers remembered better than read answers

## Duolingo Gamification Mechanics
1. **Streak System** — Daily consistency; streak freeze, streak repair; loss aversion
2. **XP Points** — Immediate reward for every action
3. **Leaderboards** — Social competition drives engagement (350% growth)
4. **Hearts/Lives** — Scarcity creates value; mistakes matter
5. **Skill Levels** — Progressive mastery; visible progress
6. **Notifications** — Personalized, humorous, well-timed reminders
7. **Streak Society** — Social belonging at milestone streaks

## Synthesis: How to Combine for Learning
- Use TikTok's vertical swipe UX for content discovery (low friction)
- Insert "knowledge checks" (active recall) between content cards
- Track engagement signals to build SM-2-like review schedule
- Interleave topics in the feed (don't just show one topic)
- Use Duolingo-style streaks + XP for habit formation
- Show forgetting curve visualization to motivate review
- Adapt difficulty based on quiz performance (ZPD)
- Use micro-content (3-5 min videos, 2-3 min articles) as primary format
- Generate "review cards" from previously consumed content
- Time-of-day optimization based on user's peak learning hours
